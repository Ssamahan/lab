# Rwanda district boundaries

`rwanda-districts.geojson` is a simplified derivative of geoBoundaries gbOpen Rwanda ADM2 (30 districts), geoBoundaries commit 9469f09, sourced from Open Data Rwanda / National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. Original data: https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/RWA/ADM2 ; full ADM2 file retrieved from https://github.com/TontonAviella/ingabe/tree/main/src/database/seed_data/geoboundaries . Attribution: geoBoundaries, Open Data Rwanda and NISR. License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Geometry was simplified with Shapely tolerance 0.001 degrees, preserving topology; properties were reduced to district name and an interior representative point. These boundaries and points are approximate and not suitable for administrative decisions. Kigali's three districts are displayed as one reporting group, not as a new official district.

The Ruli District Hospital marker uses the approximate OpenStreetMap position 29.84939° E, 1.82962° S (https://mapcarta.com/W1226961970); verify on site before operational use. The arrows join this reference point to district representative points, not to confirmed venues or transport routes.
