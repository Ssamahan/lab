What the console runs

Five working groups, fifteen screens. Everything below is the register the department already keeps on paper — carried forward, not re-typed.

Operations

Command center, referred samples with barcode custody, stock, lots, expiry and cold chain.

Scheduling

Monthly working plan, weekly roster with posts by head-count, leave, nights and a printable sheet.

Quality

EQA / PT scores and z-scores, ISO 15189 readiness, SOP control, non-conformances, equipment up to the last service.

People

Training roster and FIFO competency queue, certificates, and the competency history behind them.

District PBF

Question-level marks, quarterly visits per health centre, six weighted domains, an index out of 100%.

Referral network

Samples leave these health centres for Ruli DH and are referred on to the national reference laboratories. Every movement is timestamped at the counter.

Quality and continuity

ISO 15189 aligned

Document control, competencies, corrective actions and equipment maintenance recorded against the standard, not beside it.

Offline by design

The register lives on the device. Install it as an app, keep working through a power or network cut, sync when it returns.

No invented numbers

History, EQA scores and PBF indices derive only from recorded records. A month with no plan stays visibly empty.

Audit trail

Every change is written to an append-only trail with who, when and what — including every automatic roster decision.
